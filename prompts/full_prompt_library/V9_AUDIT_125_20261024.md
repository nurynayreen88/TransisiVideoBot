- 30 early pilot records use an earlier field schema and do not have every field used in the newer V9 files. Any loader must explicitly support both schemas or normalize them before activation. Do not infer that a structurally parseable JSON record is runtime-ready.

## Release-blocking corrections
1. Rewrite Part 2 for each affected concept with an explicit product-specific physical action, continuity anchor and non-repeated payoff. Avoid stock opening sentences.
2. Normalize the older pilot schema in a non-destructive derived manifest or exclude those records from release until independently approved; preserve original files and IDs.
3. Verify real lip-sync with visible mouth for MODEL_SPEECH, and keep PARTIAL_MODEL/HANDS_ONLY/ANIMAL_ONLY silent or off-camera voiced only.
4. Verify 10s pacing and 20s two-part continuity in actual video generation. Structural checks cannot prove rendered-video quality.
5. Resolve `{{PRODUCT_NAME}}` before user-facing emission and ground the final product identity in the uploaded image.
6. Keep PR #157 draft. Do not merge, activate engine or deploy Render on the strength of record count alone.
