# Klippo — Affiliate video prompt quality standard

Each variation must create a **different shootable 10-second storyboard**, not merely a renamed ID or synonym. Keep the product reference as the source of truth. Do not invent material, performance, functions, accessories, reverse-side details, labels, branding or testimonials.

## Variation differentiation
- **Hook (0–2 s):** a distinct visual entry that makes the product legible immediately; no generated on-screen words.
- **Action (2–6 s):** one physically plausible product-specific beat. Camera-only motion when reference does not establish moving parts or handling.
- **Detail (6–8 s):** a visible, verifiable distinguishing feature; if not supplied, choose an observable silhouette/edge/texture rather than inventing specifications.
- **Closing (8–10 s):** full unchanged product and a compelling visual resolution with a grounded contact shadow.
- **Camera:** specify shot size, movement, lens perspective and continuity; no impossible unseen surfaces.
- **Lighting:** a deliberate product-appropriate setup, not a generic repeated phrase.
- **Audio:** unobtrusive licensed-style music direction, with natural sound only where supported; no unsupported spoken claims.

A unique variation must meaningfully change at least the hook, action, camera progression and closing composition while remaining faithful to the actual product. Safety/identity constraints can repeat across entries; seven identical creative fields cannot count as different variations.

## Repair protocol
1. Preserve existing IDs, variation_number, category order, file names, and 20 records per staging file.
2. Audit exact seven-field creative duplicates across master and staging; inspect their category and visible product constraints.
3. Rewrite duplicate records with **specific, filmable** visual sequences and meaningful diversity. Never inject an arbitrary ID, adjective or number just to bypass a similarity check.
4. Re-run complete audit, inspect near-duplicate families, and merge only after substantive duplicates are resolved.
5. Do not deploy Render as part of this content audit.
